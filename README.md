# Indian IPR & Corporate Law Research Hub
**An independent legal research and knowledge repository focused on Indian Intellectual Property Rights and Corporate Law.**

## About the Project

The Indian IPR & Corporate Law Research Hub is an academic and research-oriented initiative designed to organise and document Indian legal developments, statutory frameworks, landmark judicial decisions and regulatory updates.

The repository aims to make legal research more accessible through structured case briefs, statutory analysis, comparative research and explanatory notes.

## Research Areas

### 1. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
- Trademark Law
- Copyright Law
- Patent Law
- Industrial Designs
- Geographical Indications
- Trade Secrets
- Technology and Intellectual Property

### 2. Corporate Law
- Companies Act, 2013
- Corporate Governance
- Mergers and Acquisitions
- Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
- Competition Law
- Securities and Capital Markets
- Corporate Compliance

## Research Methodology

Each research contribution will aim to include:

1. Relevant statutory provisions.
2. Background and material facts.
3. Legal issues involved.
4. Arguments and judicial reasoning.
5. Final judgment or legal position.
6. Practical implications.
7. Primary and secondary sources.

## Objectives

- Develop a structured database of Indian legal research.
- Analyse important judicial precedents.
- Track statutory amendments and regulatory developments.
- Document emerging issues in IPR and Corporate Law.
- Promote accessible and academically rigorous legal research.

## Disclaimer

This repository is intended exclusively for academic and informational purposes. Its contents do not constitute legal advice. Statutory provisions, judgments and regulatory developments should be independently verified against authoritative sources.
